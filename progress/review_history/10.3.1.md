#### Findings
1. `README.md:240-245` says the program breaks circular wait because every coder asks for its two dongles in lower-index order, then describes a ring of coders each holding one dongle. In `coders/src/dongle/take_dongles.c:85-97`, a coder marks **both** dongles unavailable only after both pass readiness checks while both mutexes are held. When either is unavailable, it waits without owning a dongle. The lower-index rule orders mutex acquisition; the atomic pair acquisition prevents a coder from holding one dongle while waiting for the other. The README should distinguish those two mechanisms and identify the Coffman condition it actually removes.
2. `README.md:260-262` says burnout occurs when **more than** `time_to_burnout` milliseconds have passed. `coders/src/monitor/get_burned_out.c:32` detects burnout at **at least** the configured interval (`last <= now - time_to_burnout`). This matters at the exact deadline, including the README's 400 ms example.

#### Requested changes
1. Correct the deadlock and Coffman explanation to match atomic pair acquisition and ordered mutex locking.
2. State that burnout is detected when the elapsed interval reaches `time_to_burnout`.
