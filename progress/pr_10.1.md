## Overview

Task 10.1 integrates the one-time startup offset into coder execution. Even seats wait briefly before their first dongle request, giving non-neighbouring coders an opportunity to enter arbitration first and compile in parallel. The existing FIFO/EDF arbitration and later coder cycles remain unchanged. The offset is a heuristic and does not promise a particular thread schedule.

## Approach

Subtask 10.1.1 introduced `sleep_start_turn`, the `START_TURN_MS` tuning macro, and the build/header integration. Subtask 10.1.2 calls that helper once after initializing local held state and before entering the request loop. A stopped or failed wait bypasses the loop and passes through the established `release_run` cleanup path. Deterministic acceptance cases check request ordering and error routing; parallel-start runs are recorded as empirical diagnostics.

## Decisions

The user chose a one-time 1 ms delay for even seats after observing serial startup. The phase review required scheduling-dependent outcomes to remain diagnostic rather than mandatory acceptance results. The README update for 10.1.2 is performed at close under the living-file rule.

## Issues resolved

**Scheduling assumption in startup criteria.** The phase gate found that earlier 10.1.2 acceptance criteria depended on odd threads running within a 1 ms window, which is not guaranteed and could fail on a loaded machine without a product defect. The phase plan was revised to use direct stopped-state and injected clock-error cases to establish that the offset precedes requests and handles errors, while keeping parallel-start runs as diagnostics. The change touched the phase 10 plan/spec and dedicated acceptance oracle; the phase gate was rerun and approved.

**Task-level verification evidence gaps.** The phase gate also identified inaccurate viability-boundary values, an unsupported interleaved-ticket claim, a diagnostic-only acceptance item, weak Valgrind evidence, and incomplete file-scope global checks across other phase 10 specs. The plan and specs were corrected to use actual boundary values, avoid inferring ticket order from logs, require meaningful checks and real tool execution, and distinguish file-scope data from function-local statics. These changes were re-reviewed and sealed in the approved phase 10 breakdown; they are included in this task branch's planning commit `1a3e6b2`.

## Issues open

The startup offset remains a heuristic. The recorded below-bound scenario still burned out in 1/10 runs; the plan makes no survival guarantee below its sufficient bound.
